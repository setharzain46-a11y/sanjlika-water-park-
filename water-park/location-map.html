How to Get Here

Sanjalika Water Park is easily accessible from all major highways and public transportation routes.

By Car

From downtown, take Highway 95 North and exit at 32 (Aquatic Drive). Follow signs to the water park.

Free parking available!

By Bus

Take bus route 105 or 220 to the Waterfront City Terminal. Transfer to route 12 which stops right at our entrance.

Bus schedule available online.

By Train

The nearest train station is Waterfront Central, just 2 miles from the park. Free shuttle service available every 30 minutes.
